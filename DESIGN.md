# Design — Our Fridays

This file was written before any image, audio or video was generated. The git history shows the order.

## The story

Two friends, Rahel and Bekele, meet for coffee every Friday. Bekele loves her and has never said so. Rahel takes a job in another city. He almost tells her, deletes the message, and helps her to the bus instead. She gives him a packet of coffee "so we can still have our Fridays", and the film ends on a promise to keep meeting.

It is fiction, written by the author for this project. No real person is shown or voiced.

## Audience

Young Ethiopian adults: the author's friends and cohort colleagues. They watch on a phone, vertically, usually with sound on but often in a noisy place, which is why the English subtitles are burned in.

## Intent

At the end the viewer should recognise the feeling of something important left unsaid, and leave warmed that the friendship continues. The film does not treat Bekele's silence as a tragedy, and it does not suggest Rahel owes him romance. Her choices are her own: she takes the job, she admits she is scared, and she is the one who proposes keeping their Fridays.

## Language

Narration in Amharic, the audience's first language, with English subtitles. An English narration track is also generated so the two can be compared.

## Why each shot exists

| Shot | What we see | Why it is in the film |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | The café; she slides his coffee over | Establish their comfortable, familiar friendship |
| 02 | He notices her tired smile | Show his attention and introduce his unspoken feelings |
| 03 | She tells him about the job | Introduce the change that puts the friendship under pressure |
| 04 | At night, his thumb over "send" | Make the unsaid confession tangible |
| 05 | He deletes the message | Show the cost of his hesitation |
| 06 | The bus station; he carries her bag | Let Rahel express her own feelings while showing Bekele's care |
| 07 | She hands him a packet of coffee | Show her active choice to keep the friendship alive |
| 08 | The bus leaves; "Friday. Same time?" | Resolve the separation with a promise, leaving his feelings unspoken |

The same reasons are stored per shot in `storyboard.json` as `reason`, and validation fails if one is missing.
