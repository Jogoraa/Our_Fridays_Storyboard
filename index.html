The Window መስኮቱ

Two old men share a room in a home for the elderly. One has the bed by the only window, and every afternoon he describes the world outside to the friend who cannot sit up.

Adapted from the parable “The Hospital Window”. Narrated in Amharic with English subtitles. 81 seconds.

    Nothing here was edited by hand. One command, uv run film, turns the storyboard into narration, pictures, a score and both films. Every model call leaves one line in runs.jsonl; the table below is computed from that file as you load this page.
